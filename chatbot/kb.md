# Falak Hesab — help knowledge base

Falak Hesab (falakhesab.com) is online double-entry accounting for small businesses and travel agencies. It is made by Falak Travel in Istanbul, Turkey. It works in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers; there is nothing to install. Languages: English, Dari, Pashto and Turkish — each person picks their own language in the language menu (bottom of the left menu, or on the sign-in page).

Support: WhatsApp +93 79 104 4260, email falaktravelagency11@gmail.com, or "Help & Support" at the bottom of the left menu. Tutorial videos exist for: getting started, customers and invoices, bank and cash, purchases, travel, reports, team and settings, safety and backups.

## Plans and prices (per business, per month)
- Free: $0 / 0 TL. 1 user. All accounting: sales, purchases, bank and cash, reports, inventory, payroll, assets, backups and history. Printed documents show a small "Made with Falak Hesab" line.
- Basic: $9 / 299 TL per month. Up to 3 users. Everything in Free, plus a company address with your logo: falakhesab.com/your-company. No "Made with" line.
- Pro: $19 / 649 TL per month. Unlimited users. Everything in Basic, plus Travel (tickets, airlines, sub-agents).
- New accounts choose a plan when they sign up. Basic and Pro can be tried free for 30 days, once per owner. No bank card is needed.
- Payment: by bank transfer or in cash, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months in advance. No automatic charges. In the app open Business → Plan & billing, choose Basic or Pro, follow the payment instructions and send the receipt on WhatsApp. The plan is switched on when the payment is received.
- When a trial or paid plan (Basic or Pro) ends: nothing is deleted. The books become read-only for everyone: nobody can add or change records (invoices, payments, tickets, customers...) or add attachments. Everyone can still open and print everything, run every report, export lists and reports (CSV) and download a full backup from Settings. Users the owner added keep view-only access; users can be removed but not added. The company address keeps working. Paying for Basic or Pro opens everything again as before.
- Switching to Free: only the owner can do it (Plan & billing → Choose Free), and only after removing the other users, because Free is for 1 user. The company address is removed and printed documents show a small "Made with Falak Hesab" line. All accounting keeps working. Existing travel tickets are kept: they can be opened, printed, exported, appear in reports, and payments can be recorded for them, but tickets cannot be added or changed (Travel needs Pro). The free trial is only once per owner.
- On Basic, travel tickets are also read-only (Travel needs Pro); Basic allows up to 3 users.
- Prices may change; the current prices are always on falakhesab.com and in Plan & billing.

## Getting started
1. On falakhesab.com press "Try free" (or "Sign in" if you have an account).
2. Choose a plan (Free, Basic trial or Pro trial), type your email and a password (at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers), press "Create account".
3. Open the confirmation email and click the link (check the spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes).
4. Sign in. On the Businesses page press "+ New business": type the business name, the main currency (for example USD, TRY, EUR, AFN) and the start date of your books. Tick "Add example customers and invoices" to practise with sample data (you can delete the business later).
5. You land on the Summary: balance sheet and profit and loss.
- Demo without an account (sample data, stays only in that browser, starts with a short guided tour): small business falakhesab.com/?demo=business, travel agency falakhesab.com/?demo=travel. "Take the tour" in the yellow demo bar restarts the tour; "Start over" resets the sample data.
- New businesses show a "Get your business ready" checklist on the Summary for owners and administrators: check the base currency, add opening balances (bank and cash accounts with their balance on the start date, and what customers and suppliers owed), first customer, first sales invoice, first payment received. It can be hidden with "Hide this list".

## Signing in problems
- Forgot password: on the sign-in page press "Forgot password?", type your email, open the email and set a new password.
- No confirmation email: wait a few minutes, check spam/junk, make sure the email address is correct; then contact support.
- "Invalid login": check the email and password, Caps Lock, and that the email was confirmed.
- Two-step login: if you turned it on, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. If you lost the phone with the app, contact support from the account email.
- Company address (falakhesab.com/your-company): staff sign in there with their own email and password. If it says the address does not exist, check the spelling or ask the business owner.
- "You don't have access to this business": the owner must add your email in Users & Access (and you must sign up with exactly that email).
- Falak Hesab signs you out after 60 minutes without activity on shared computers, for safety.
- Change password: My account (bottom of the left menu) → new password → Change password.

## The screen
- Left menu groups: Overview (Summary, Reports), Travel (Pro), Money (Bank & Cash Accounts, Receipts, Payments, Inter Account Transfers), Sales (Customers, Sales Quotes, Sales Orders, Sales Invoices, Recurring Invoices, Credit Notes, Delivery Notes), Purchases (Suppliers, Purchase Orders, Purchase Invoices, Debit Notes, Goods Receipts), Inventory (Inventory Items, Inventory Write-offs), Payroll (Employees, Payslips, Expense Claims), Assets (Fixed Assets, Depreciation Entries), Accounting (Journal Entries, Projects, Chart of Accounts, Tax Codes, Currencies), Business (Settings, Users & Access, Plan & billing, History).
- Settings → Customize tabs hides tabs you do not use.
- The "Businesses" button (top right) switches between businesses. One account can own or join many businesses.
- Every list has Search and Export (CSV for Excel). Every document has Print / PDF.

## Sales
- Customers → "+ New customer": name, email, phone/WhatsApp, address, payment terms (days to pay), currency, starting balance.
- Sales Invoices → "+ New sales invoice": choose the customer; issue date, due date and number are filled in. On each line choose an item or type a description, quantity, unit price, account and tax code. Totals and tax are calculated. Press "Create".
- On the invoice: "Receive payment" (creates a receipt filled in for you), "Credit note", "Delivery note", "Make recurring", "Share" (a link the customer can open), "Edit", "Print / PDF".
- Status shows Due, Paid or Overdue.
- Sales Quotes and Sales Orders: open one and use "Copy to sales invoice" (a quote can also be copied to a sales order). Purchase Orders have "Copy to purchase invoice".
- Recurring Invoices create invoices automatically on a schedule (for example monthly).
- Credit Notes reduce what a customer owes (returns, discounts).
- Customer statements: Reports → Customer statements → choose the customer; print, PDF or send on WhatsApp.
- Aged receivables report shows who owes you and how late.

## Purchases
- Suppliers → "+ New supplier".
- Purchase Invoices → "+ New purchase invoice": supplier, lines (item, quantity, cost). Stock items increase inventory; the amount is added to what you owe the supplier.
- "Pay this invoice" on a purchase invoice creates the payment filled in for you.
- Debit Notes reduce what you owe a supplier. Goods Receipts record goods received. Purchase Orders are sent to suppliers.
- Aged payables report shows what you owe and for how long.

## Money
- Bank & Cash Accounts: every bank account and cash box with its balance. Click one to see every movement like a bank statement.
- Receipts = money in. Payments = money out. On each line choose what the money was for (an account like Rent, or a customer/supplier and invoice).
- Inter Account Transfers move money between your own accounts (for example bank to cash).
- Import statement: in a bank account, import a CSV bank statement. Reconcile: tick transactions that cleared the bank.
- Many currencies: each bank account, customer and supplier can have its own currency; amounts are converted to the base currency in reports.

## Travel (Pro plan)
- Travel overview: tickets, sales, airline costs and profit for a period; upcoming flights (next 7 days) with a WhatsApp reminder for each passenger; what each airline and sub-agent owes; unpaid tickets; profit by route.
- Travel overview warnings at the top: Overdue from sub-agents, Due to airlines in the next 7 days, Open refunds (click a tile to jump to its table).
- "Sub-agents: what they owe and how late": each sub-agent's unpaid tickets split into Not due yet, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days late, the oldest unpaid ticket and a Statement button.
- "Airline payments due": per airline what is overdue, due in 7 days and due later, the next due date, deposit left and a Pay button. "Deposit running low" shows when an airline's deposit is less than the last 7 days of ticket costs.
- Due dates: a ticket sold to a sub-agent is due after that agent's Payment terms (days); the airline cost after the airline's Payment terms (set in the supplier/customer details; 0 = due on the issue date). Walk-in customers pay on the issue date.
- "Refunds" table: every cancelled ticket with its status (Pay back customer, Waiting for airline refund, Done) and buttons to pay the refund or receive it from the airline.
- Tickets → "+ New ticket": passenger, PNR, ticket number, airline (a supplier marked as airline), route, flight and return dates, sold to (walk-in customer or sub-agent), selling price, cost from airline. Profit is calculated. "Refunded / cancelled" tick box.
- The ticket receipt never shows the cost to the customer. Print it or send it on WhatsApp. "Receive payment" from the ticket.
- Airlines and sub-agents can have deposits ("Add deposit").

## Inventory, payroll, assets, accounting
- Inventory Items: stock items with quantity and cost; sales reduce stock, purchases increase it. Write-offs for damaged/lost stock. Reports → Inventory value.
- Employees, Payslips (salary, deductions), Expense Claims (staff paid with their own money). Payslips are private: only people allowed to see Payslips can see them.
- Fixed Assets and Depreciation Entries.
- Journal Entries for manual double-entry adjustments (debits must equal credits).
- Projects: tag income and expenses to see profit per project (Reports → Profit and loss by project).
- Chart of Accounts: add or rename accounts. Tax Codes: for example KDV 20%. Currencies: add foreign currencies and rates.

## Reports
- Summary: balance sheet and profit and loss; change From/To dates and press Apply.
- Reports page includes: Trial balance, General ledger summary, Profit and loss by month, Profit and loss by project, Aged receivables, Aged payables, Inventory value, Tax summary, Cash flow statement, Statement of changes in equity, Balance sheet – comparison, Profit and loss – comparison, Sales by customer, Receipts and payments summary, Customer statements, Supplier statements.
- Every report: Print / PDF and Export (CSV for Excel).

## Team and settings
- Settings → Business details: name, base currency, start date, lock date, phone, email, tax number, address and default invoice notes (printed on documents). Upload your logo. Footers. Custom fields (your own boxes, for example passport number on customers or tickets; can show in lists and on printed documents).
- Company address (Basic and Pro): Settings → Company address, for example falakhesab.com/my-company: your own sign-in page with your logo for staff.
- Users & Access → "+ Add user": type the staff member's email and choose an access level: Administrator (everything), Limited user (only the tabs you tick), View only (can look, cannot change). Then send them the sign-in link ("Copy link" or "Send on WhatsApp"). They create a free account with the same email and see the business.
- Free plan: 1 user only. Basic: up to 3 users. Pro: unlimited.

## Safety and backups
- Attachments: on any invoice, receipt, payment, ticket etc. press "Add files" or drag files (photos, PDF, Word, Excel, up to 10 MB each).
- History: every change, who made it and when. Recycle bin: deleted items can be restored for 30 days.
- Lock date (Settings): no changes on or before that date.
- Backup: Settings → Backup → "Download backup" (a file with all your books; attachments are not inside it). "Import backup" on the Businesses page restores it as a business.
- Two-step login: My account → "Turn on two-step login" → scan the QR code with Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator → type the code.
- The database is backed up automatically every day (kept 7 days). Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL); the website is hosted by Netlify. Passwords are encrypted by the sign-in provider; Falak Hesab staff never see them.
- Delete a business: Settings → Delete business (owner only).

## Common problems
- Page looks wrong or old after an update: refresh the page (Ctrl+F5 / pull down on phone).
- "Your plan has ended" / cannot save: open Plan & billing to pay or switch to Free.
- "Only Pro" on Travel: Travel needs the Pro plan.
- Cannot add a user: Free allows 1 user; choose Basic or Pro.
- Cannot change an old transaction: it is on or before the lock date; an owner/administrator can change the lock date in Settings.
- A limited user cannot see a tab: the owner must tick that tab in Users & Access.
- Totals look wrong: check dates (From/To), currency, and whether transactions are dated before the start date of books.
